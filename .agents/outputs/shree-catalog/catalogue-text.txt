Research basis
The taxonomy was cross-checked against current online Indian/Mumbai sources covering electrical supplies, plumbing pipes and fittings, hand and power tools, fasteners, paints, safety, bathroom products, building materials, furniture hardware and related home-improvement ranges. The goal is a broad local-store catalogue, not a verified live-inventory feed. Replit implementation rule
Create Category records with id, name, representativeImage and description. Create Product records with id, categoryId, name, specification, price, stock, brand (optional) and image. Initially assign the same representativeImage to every product in its category. This gives the site thousands of product names without requiring thousands of separate photos.
